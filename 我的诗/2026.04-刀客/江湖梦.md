绿衣轻，红衫灵，笑问江湖事。
山露寒，竹雾深，眉间一缕愁。
星有意，月无情，挥剑为哪般？
两鬓老，华发生，天地我独行。

---

Chinese cinematic duet, male and female vocal alternating then harmonizing, mid-tempo 78 BPM, key in D minor. Opening with guzheng and bamboo flute, soft piano and light percussion. Verse 1: female vocal gentle and wistful. Verse 2: male vocal deeper and weary. Chorus: both voices blend, strings swell, erhu solo for emotional bridge. Dynamic contrast between intimate verses and powerful chorus. Ending with solo flute fading out. Traditional Chinese folk ballad style with modern orchestral touch. Lyrics theme: regret, memory, shared dream of jianghu that time erased.
