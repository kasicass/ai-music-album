[Genre] Chinese Ancient Style Ballad, Cinematic Folk

[Tempo] 72 BPM, slow and flowing

[Key] D minor → F major modulation at chorus

[Instrumentation]
- Main: Guzheng (glissando, tremolo technique), Dizi (soft breathy tone)
- Rhythm: Muted acoustic guitar fingerpicking, light Taiko drum (brush stroke)
- Atmosphere: Gentle wind chimes, distant water waves field recording
- Chorus layer: Soft erhu (long sustains), male chorus (humming "ahh")

[Vocal Style] Male tenor, breathy and melancholic, slight vibrato on long notes, whispering quality at phrase endings

[Arrangement Structure]
- Intro: 8 bars (guzheng arpeggio + water waves)
- Verse 1: Guzheng + guitar (low dynamics)
- Verse 2: Add dizi countermelody
- Chorus: Full ensemble, taiko enters (forte)
- Interlude: Erhu solo (16 bars)
- Verse 3: Return to sparse texture
- Chorus repeat with vocal chorus
- Outro: Fade with guzheng tremolo and wind chime

[Production Notes]
- Reverb: Large hall, 3.5s decay
- Mix: Wide stereo with guzheng center, dizi panned 35% L, erhu 40% R
- Master: -14 LUFS, gentle high-shelf cut (-2dB at 8kHz) for vintage warmth

[Emotion Keywords] Nostalgia, longing, dreamlike, bittersweet, riverside moonlight

---

主歌1：
钱塘两岸夜江潮
把酒与君共摇
月色浸透旧时袍
马蹄声远渐消

主歌2：
山河依稀入梦遥
烟波深处闻箫
当年仗剑踏青霄
如今霜落眉梢

副歌：
涛声惊起白沙鹭
北燕南飞影寂寥
问君何时归故道
共饮此杯天地老

此生一曲终了
红尘梦里笑
若君归来花正好
莫让相思空对桥

主歌3：
残灯孤影照寒宵
梦里故园春草
醒时江月已落潮
唯有风声如刀

副歌重复：
涛声惊起白沙鹭
北燕南飞影寂寥
问君何时归故道
共饮此杯天地老

尾声：
此生一曲终了
把酒与君…梦已遥
